The Candidate

Meet Dominque Yorke

Born and raised in Barbados, Dominque Yorke isn't just a politician—she is a mother, a community advocate, and a fierce defender of democratic values.

For over a decade, she has worked tirelessly on the ground in Christ Church East Central, listening to families and fighting for the resources they deserve. Now, as the representative for the Friends of Democracy, she is ready to take that fight to Parliament.

Her vision is simple: A government that listens, an economy that works for everyone, and a community that leaves no one behind.

Our Vision

Building the Future

Concrete solutions for the challenges we face today.

Economic Renewal

Empowering small businesses and creating green jobs through targeted tax relief and infrastructure investment.

Youth & Education

Modernizing our schools and providing vocational training to ensure every young person has a path to success.

Sustainability

Protecting our natural heritage with a bold plan for renewable energy and waste reduction.

Justice & Safety

Reforming our justice system and increasing community policing to keep our neighborhoods safe.

Healthcare Access

Fighting for affordable prescription drugs and reduced wait times at local polyclinics.

Transparency

Upholding the core values of the Friends of Democracy by ensuring every dollar of public money is accounted for.
